About Our Game

Our game was made for our Studio 1 class with oversight from Ghost Gaming. We've concidered existing Fortnite games that have done well and balanced that with our own goals of what we wanted to create. Fortnite has many built-it movement options for players. Wall kicks, grabbing ledges, sprinting, boosted jumps
